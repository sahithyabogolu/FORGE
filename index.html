METHOD

Transparent by design.

PDF and DOCX files are converted to text locally in this browser. CSV and Excel sheets are read locally. FORGE looks for standard consolidated-statement labels and values near them. It does not upload, store, or send the document to a server.

  • Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities
  • Debt/equity = total debt ÷ shareholders’ equity
  • Gross margin = gross profit ÷ revenue
  • Net margin = net income ÷ revenue
  • Cash conversion = operating cash flow ÷ net income
