# Forge
A premium, minimalist financial clarity tool — built for founders juggling cash flow, rising costs, and too many hats. Also reads 10-Ks and helps investors decide whether a company is worth backing.

Built by **Sahithya** and **Yukti**.

## What it does

- **Cash Flow** — stay ahead of your cash position, always
- **Cost Watch** — catch rising operational costs before they bite
- **Statement Scanner** — upload any file format and instantly scan and understand:
  - Balance Sheet
  - Income Statement (Profit & Loss)
  - Cash Flow Statement
  - Statement of Shareholders' Equity

  For each statement, get a clear read on what matters — company position, financial health, and key risks or red flags to know before making a call.

- **10-K Reader & Investor Analysis** — upload a 10-K and get a straight answer on whether the company looks investment-worthy, backed by the numbers that matter
- **Forecast Simulator** — weekly and monthly projections, built from your own numbers

## How it works

1. Enter your name
2. Pick a feature
3. Upload a statement or 10-K, or enter your numbers
4. Get instant clarity, analysis, and a forecast

## Tech

Runs fully in-browser. No backend, no API keys, no server setup. Works anywhere, on any connection.
